Ordering Guide

  1. Select Items: Choose items from the Menu or Deals section.
  2. Payment: Pay at the counter via cash or student card.
  3. Token Collection: Receive your order token from the cashier.
  4. Pickup: Collect your food when your token number is announced.

Guidelines & Rules

⏰ Peak Hours

Peak hours are between 12:30 PM and 2:00 PM. Please maintain queues.

🎫 Keep Your Token

Orders are handed over only against the token number.

🧼 Clean Up

Please return trays and leave tables tidy for the next student.
